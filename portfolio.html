About Me

get to know the developer behind the code

My name is Paul Ames, and I was born in Canton, NY on June 15th, 2009. My father’s name is Dennis, and my mother’s name is Cassandra. I also have a sister named Autumn. My technical skills are limited. I was inspired to take a Computer Information Sciences course because I find programming fascinating, and I want to learn coding languages. I went to Saint Mary’s from kindergarten to 5th grade, then I went to Canton Central School for middle school and high school. I have a cat named Jack who is very entertaining because he is not the most intelligent creature and chases his tail. I met my best friend Ethan in 9th grade because I changed my schedule and had a few classes with him. I am involved in Future Farmers of America (FFA), which involves team building. I plan on going to Suny Canton in the future for a programming class to further my knowledge. In ten years I see myself working a job that has something to do with programming.
